Why isn’t FMM faster? Three force models, three costs: direct O(N²) self-gravity is impossible per frame at 5M (25×10¹² pairs ≈ seconds even on a 4070); “None” is the analytic two-core O(N) formula (real physics replaced by two point masses — no particle-particle forces, no tidal response of one disk on the other); FMM is true particle-particle gravity in O(N) (Build+M2L+L2P ≈ 7.5 ms at 5M). Measured at 5M on a 4070-class adapter (GPU_NOTES.md §4): Render ≈ 1.8 ms (5M points are cheap for the rasterizer, as expected), FMM chain ≈ 7.5 ms, and the P2P near-field pass (“Main Compute”) ≈ 59 ms — 86% of the frame. That pass is slow not from math but from millions of dependent, uncoalesced gathers through the cell lists; the round-6 leaf auto-tune targets exactly this pass.
